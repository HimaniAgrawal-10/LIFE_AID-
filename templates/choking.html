😷 Choking Emergency

If the person cannot breathe or speak, act immediately.

Symptoms

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Unable to speak
  • Hands around throat
  • Blue lips

First Aid Steps

  1. Encourage coughing.
  2. Give 5 back blows.
  3. Perform abdominal thrusts if trained.
  4. Call emergency services.
  5. Continue until help arrives.
