🔥 Burns Emergency

Cool the burn immediately with clean running water for at least 10 minutes.

Symptoms

  • Red skin
  • Blisters
  • Swelling
  • Pain

First Aid Steps

  1. Cool the burn under running water.
  2. Remove tight items near the burn.
  3. Cover with sterile gauze.
  4. Do not apply ice.
  5. Seek medical help if severe.
