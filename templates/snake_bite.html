🐍 Snake Bite Emergency

Keep the victim calm and seek medical attention immediately.

Symptoms

  • Pain at bite site
  • Swelling
  • Nausea
  • Difficulty breathing

First Aid Steps

  1. Keep the person still.
  2. Keep the bite below heart level.
  3. Remove tight jewellery.
  4. Do not cut or suck the wound.
  5. Reach a hospital immediately.
