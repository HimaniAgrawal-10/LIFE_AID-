🩸 Bleeding Emergency

Heavy bleeding can become life-threatening within minutes.

Symptoms

  • Continuous blood loss
  • Dizziness
  • Weakness
  • Pale skin

First Aid Steps

  1. Apply direct pressure.
  2. Use a clean cloth or bandage.
  3. Elevate the injured area if possible.
  4. Do not remove soaked bandages.
  5. Seek emergency assistance.
