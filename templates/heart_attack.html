❤️ Heart Attack Emergency

Emergency Warning: Call ambulance immediately if the person has severe chest pain, breathing difficulty, or unconsciousness.

Symptoms

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Shortness of breath
  • Sweating
  • Nausea
  • Pain in arm, neck or jaw

First Aid Steps

  1. Call emergency services immediately.
  2. Keep the patient calm and seated.
  3. Loosen tight clothing.
  4. If advised by a medical professional, give aspirin.
  5. Monitor breathing until help arrives.
Do not delay medical treatment. Early response can save lives.
